# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We welcome everyone regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback and focus on what benefits the community.
- Show empathy and take responsibility for mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized language, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project issues, pull requests, and other community spaces, including when representing the project publicly.

Report concerns to the repository owner through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Do not disclose sensitive information in public issues. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporter privacy, and may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation for violations.
